Every team that asks me to help them "do AI" eventually asks a different question: why does our data look different every time someone pulls a report?

That's a governance problem, not a modeling problem. Fix that first.

## Where to start

- Agree on one source of truth per metric, in writing.
- Automate the boring reconciliation checks before automating anything interesting.
- Treat data quality bugs with the same urgency as production incidents.
