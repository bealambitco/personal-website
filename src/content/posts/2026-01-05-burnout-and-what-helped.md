Burnout doesn't announce itself. It shows up as irritability at things that never used to bother you, and a growing list of "I'll get to it" tasks that never gets to it.

What actually helped, in order of impact:

1. A hard stop time, enforced by a calendar block, not willpower.
2. Saying no to the fourth "quick call" of the day.
